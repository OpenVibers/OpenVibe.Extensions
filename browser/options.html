OpenVibe Companion

Coupon codes, the Rent board and Watch all work without connecting. Connecting lets the Companion report whether a code worked, as you.

  1. On openvibe.coupons/connect-extension, sign in and create a token for this browser.
  2. Paste it here. It can only look up codes and report on them; it is not your OpenVibe sign-in.

Removes the token from this browser. Revoke it on openvibe.coupons as well.

Needed to report a code and to read your Dock; the Companion never asks for access to other sites.

The Dock in the side panel

The Dock shows what your agents are doing, what your chats wait for and what just happened, on every site, in the side panel. It is the same Dock, and it keeps the same place.

Press Sign in once: the Network asks which account to use, and the extension keeps a read-only Dock sign-in that refreshes itself. The access token is kept in memory; the refresh token stays on this device. Sign out revokes it at the Network.

Use a pasted token instead (older connection)

The 0.7.0 way: sign in at openvibe.network, then paste the token your browser has for OpenVibe (in the page, localStorage.ov_token). This is a full OpenVibe session, not the extension's scoped sign-in; it stays on this device and is still accepted until a later release removes it.

This does not sign out the one-click connection; use Sign out for that.

What this extension can see

  • The tab you are on, and only when you open the Companion: its address and title.
  • It sends openvibe.coupons only the hostname, to find that shop’s codes.
  • When you press “Keep on my Rent board” or “Watch this page”, it opens openvibe.rent or openvibe.watch in a new tab with the page’s address and title, without its tracking parameters. You see that tab, and nothing is saved until you save it there.
  • No page content, no cookies, no browsing history, nothing in the background.
